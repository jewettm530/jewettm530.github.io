About

Computer science with a research mindset.

I’m a computer science student focused on data science and machine learning. My work is increasingly centered on healthcare AI—especially medical imaging, computational mental health, and the use of behavioral and physiological data to study human outcomes.

I enjoy projects that require more than fitting a model: cleaning difficult data, designing reproducible experiments, choosing defensible evaluation strategies, interpreting limitations, and turning technical results into something other people can actually understand.
