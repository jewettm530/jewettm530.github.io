Puberty & Virtual Reality

Research pipelines for understanding reward learning, stress responses, individual differences, and developmental patterns across behavioral and physiological data.

View repository ↗

The question

How do people learn reward contingencies, how much do those learning patterns vary between individuals, and how might learning relate to stress or developmental differences?

Trial-level behavior + heart rate + EEG + session metadata → validated participant-session dataset → learning & stress analyses

What I built

  • JATOS behavioral-data conversion and standardization
  • Raw-data inventories and schema validation
  • Group-average and individual learning curves
  • Learning-rate, slope, and win-stay / lose-switch analyses
  • Heart-rate and EEG availability/quality summaries
  • Integrated participant × session multimodal datasets
  • Developmental-group comparison workflows

Research mindset

The repository is designed around reproducibility and data quality rather than only producing a final graph. File naming, participant/session matching, missing-session handling, and modality availability are explicitly validated before downstream analysis.

Statistical caution matters: learner-group splits and small developmental subgroups are treated as exploratory, and the repository documents where stronger confirmatory models would be appropriate.

What this project demonstrates

  • Behavioral-data analysis
  • Research data engineering
  • Multimodal integration
  • Statistical inference and effect sizes
  • EEG and heart-rate data handling
  • Privacy-conscious participant data management
